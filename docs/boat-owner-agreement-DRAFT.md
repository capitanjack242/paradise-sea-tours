## 1. What this agreement is for

1. Paradise finds passengers for boat rides and charters in Nassau and its
   cays, takes their bookings and payments, and offers each run to a boat
   through the Paradise Sea Express captain app.
2. The Owner owns, or is legally entitled to operate commercially, the boat(s)
   in Schedule A, and wants to take runs from Paradise on these terms.
3. **Joining is free.** The Owner pays no fee to join. Paradise earns only its
   commission on runs the Owner completes.

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Run | A ride or charter booked through Paradise and accepted by the Owner's boat. |
| Fare | The price of a Run before VAT, tips and any promo-code discount: the published per-person fare, or the charter price Paradise quoted. |
| Commission | The share of each Fare that Paradise keeps (Section 5). |
| Captain | The licensed person in command of a boat during a Run, whether the Owner or someone the Owner engages. |
| Passenger | Anyone who books or travels on a Run. |
| Captain app | The Paradise Sea Express captain app and the captain's board at /captain/. |
| Pay week | Friday to Thursday. |

## 3. How runs work

1. **Offers.** Paradise offers Runs to the Owner's boat in the Captain app. The
   Owner may accept or decline any Run. An offer not accepted within
   **5 minutes** lapses and may go to another boat.
2. **Accepted Runs.** Once accepted and paid for by the Passenger, a Run is
   binding. The Owner turns up at the pickup point at the booked time, ready to
   go.
3. **Prices.** Paradise sets the Fares from its published fare list and quotes
   charters and unlisted routes. Neither the Owner nor the Captain negotiates
   price with Passengers.
4. **No payment on board.** Passengers pay Paradise by card before the Run. The
   Captain takes no cash or card payment for a Run on board, other than a tip
   a Passenger chooses to give in person.
5. **Not exclusive.** The Owner may run their own passengers and work with
   others. When doing so, the Owner switches the Captain app off so no Runs are
   offered.
6. **Paradise's Passengers.** [ADMIN: decide whether to include] For
   [6] months after a Run, the Owner will not solicit that Passenger for a
   direct booking of the same route to avoid the Commission. Passengers who
   approach the Owner unprompted are not covered.
7. **Messages and location.** The Owner and Captain use the Captain app's
   messaging and pickup location only to carry out Runs, and keep Passenger
   details private.

## 4. The Owner's responsibilities

The Owner is responsible for each boat and every Run, and promises that for as
long as this Agreement lasts:

1. **Right to operate.** The Owner owns each boat or has written authority to
   operate it commercially, and no lender or co-owner prevents it.
2. **Licences.** Each boat holds every registration, commercial licence and
   inspection Bahamian law requires for carrying paying passengers on the
   routes it runs [ADMIN + lawyer: name them, e.g. the Port Department /
   Bahamas Maritime Authority commercial licence].
3. **Captains.** Every Captain holds a valid licence for the boat, the number
   of passengers and the waters used, and is sober and fit while on duty.
   Captains are engaged and paid by the Owner, not Paradise.
4. **Boat condition.** Each boat is seaworthy, clean, maintained, and carries
   all required safety gear, including a properly sized life jacket for every
   person aboard.
5. **Capacity.** The Owner never carries more people than the boat's licensed
   capacity.
6. **Running costs.** Fuel, maintenance, dock fees, crew and all other costs of
   running the boat are the Owner's and come out of the Owner's share.
7. **Incidents.** The Owner tells Paradise within [ADMIN: 24] hours of any
   accident, injury, damage, collision, grounding, Passenger complaint or
   police or authority contact connected with a Run, and makes any report the
   law requires.
8. **Paperwork.** On request, the Owner gives Paradise copies of the boat's
   licences, Captain licences and insurance certificates within
   [ADMIN: 5] business days.

## 5. Money

| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Owner's share | **75%** of the Fare of every completed Run |
| Paradise Commission | **25%** of the Fare; pays for marketing, card fees, the office and the software |
| Promo-code discounts | Paid by Paradise. The Owner's 75% is worked out on the full Fare. |
| VAT | Charged to the Passenger on top of the Fare; does not come out of the Owner's share |
| Tips | **100%** to the Captain; no Commission and no VAT taken |
| Pay day | Every Friday, by bank transfer, for the Pay week that just ended |
| Currency | US dollars |

1. **Collection.** Paradise collects all Passenger payments through its
   payment provider as the Owner's limited payment agent.
2. **Statements.** The Owner and Paradise see the same list of Runs behind
   every Friday payment in the Captain app. The Owner raises any error within
   [ADMIN: 30] days.
3. **Different rate.** A Commission other than 25% for a particular boat must
   be agreed in writing and written into Schedule A.
4. **Corrections.** A Run entered or corrected after its week was paid is paid
   on the next Friday. Paradise may deduct from a future payment any amount
   paid in error or refunded to a Passenger under Section 6, after telling the
   Owner.

## 6. Cancellations, no-shows and weather

1. **No-show.** If a Passenger has not arrived after the Captain has waited
   [ADMIN: 10] minutes at the pickup point, the Passenger is charged the full
   Fare and the Owner is paid 75% of it.
2. **Late cancellation.** If a Passenger cancels once the Captain is on the way
   to the pickup, the Passenger pays a cancellation fee of [ADMIN: amount] and
   the Owner is paid 75% of it.
3. **Weather and safety.** The Captain may refuse, delay or end a Run when
   weather, sea state, a mechanical fault or a Passenger's behaviour makes it
   unsafe. [ADMIN: what the Passenger is refunded and whether the Owner is
   paid anything in that case.]
4. **Owner cancels.** If the Owner cancels an accepted Run for any other
   reason, the Passenger is refunded in full and the Owner is paid nothing for
   it. Repeated cancellations may lead Paradise to stop offering Runs to that
   boat.
5. **Breakdown.** If a boat cannot complete a Run through no fault of the
   Passenger, Paradise may send another boat, and the Owner is paid only for
   any part of the Run its boat completed.

## 7. Insurance and liability

1. **Insurance.** The Owner keeps, at its own cost, with a reputable insurer:
    - Hull cover for each boat; and
    - Passenger and third-party liability cover for commercial passenger use of
      at least [ADMIN + lawyer: amount] per incident.
2. **Paradise named.** Paradise is named as an additional insured on the
   liability policy where the insurer allows it. The Owner sends a certificate
   before taking the first Run and at each renewal, and tells Paradise straight
   away if cover lapses or is reduced.
3. **Owner indemnity.** The Owner will indemnify Paradise against claims,
   losses, fines and reasonable legal costs arising from: a boat or its
   operation; any act or omission of a Captain or crew; injury, death or damage
   during a Run; or the Owner's breach of this Agreement or the law.
4. **Paradise indemnity.** Paradise will indemnify the Owner against claims
   arising from Paradise's own negligence or misconduct in taking bookings and
   payments.
5. **Limit.** Except for the indemnities and fraud, neither Party is liable for
   indirect or consequential loss, and Paradise's total liability under this
   Agreement is limited to the Commission it earned from the Owner in the
   [ADMIN: 12] months before the claim.

## 8. Term and ending the agreement

1. This Agreement starts on the date above and continues until either Party
   ends it.
2. Either Party may end it at any time with [ADMIN: 14] days' written notice.
   Runs already accepted are completed and paid on these terms.
3. Paradise may stop offering Runs to a boat at once if its insurance or
   licences lapse, a Captain is unlicensed or unfit, a serious safety incident
   happens, or Passengers raise credible safety concerns.
4. Money owed, Section 7 and Section 9 continue after the Agreement ends.
