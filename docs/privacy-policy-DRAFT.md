## 1. What we collect from passengers

| What | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Your name | When you request a boat | So your captain knows who to look for |
| Your mobile number | When you request a boat | So we can reach you about the trip. Captains are **not** given your number |
| Trip details: pickup, destination, date and time, number of people, trip type, return time, notes | When you request a boat | To arrange and price the trip |
| Your location | **Only if you choose to share it**, once, when you request a boat in the app | So your captain can find you on the dock |
| Messages | When you write to us or your captain | To run the trip |
| Ratings and comments | If you rate a trip | To keep standards up and look after our captains |
| Payment records | When you pay or tip | To show what is paid and owed, and for tax records |

We do **not** collect or store your card number. Card payments are taken by our
payment provider, Fygaro, on its own secure page (see section 4).

We do not require an account. Your trip is reached through a private link; anyone
with that link can see the trip, so please don't share it.

## 2. What we collect from captains

Name, email and login, the boat you operate, whether you are available, your
trips, messages with passengers and the office, and your earnings and payouts.

**Your boat's position** is collected only while you are switched on as
available and the Captain app is open. Switching off deletes the last position
immediately, and every captain is switched off automatically at 10pm Nassau time.
A passenger sees your boat's position only on their own live trip, and only if
it is less than five minutes old.

## 3. How long we keep it

- **A passenger's shared location** is deleted as soon as the trip is completed
  or cancelled.
- **A boat's position** is deleted as soon as the captain switches off.
- **Trip, payment and payout records** are kept for [ADMIN: e.g. 7 years] because
  Bahamas VAT and business record-keeping rules require it.
- **Messages and ratings** are kept for [ADMIN: e.g. 2 years], then deleted.

## 4. Who else handles your information

We use a small number of service providers, only for the purposes above:

- **Supabase** — stores our database. [ADMIN: data region, from the Supabase dashboard]
- **Cloudflare** — hosts the website.
- **Fygaro** — takes card payments. Fygaro's own privacy policy applies to the
  card details you give it.
- **Telegram** — sends our office a notification of each new booking request,
  including the name, number and trip details.

  and messages.
- **Google Maps** — opens when you or a captain tap "open the map". We send only
  the coordinates.

We do not sell your information, and we don't use it for advertising.
