How a run works

Like Uber, for boats.
Except the fare is fixed and you keep 75%.

1

They book

A passenger picks the trip online or in our app and sees the fare up front.

2

We ask you

Between 7am and 10pm, the run lands on your phone with an alert: who, where, when, how many.

3

You say yes

Tap yes or no within 5 minutes. Nothing is promised to the passenger until a boat agrees.

4

Run it, get paid

Pick them up, take them out, mark it done. Paid that Friday week.

Paradise Sea Express · For captains & boat owners · 3

Stress that the passenger is not told “confirmed” until a captain has said yes, so no one is stranded and no captain is pushed into a trip. If you don’t answer in five minutes, the office can ask another boat.
