The deal

Simple terms, and every run on your phone

75%

of every fare is yours

Paradise keeps 25% for marketing, card payments and the office. Your fuel and running costs come out of your 75%.

100%

of every tip is yours

No commission and no VAT taken from tips, ever. Passengers can tip in the app after the ride.

Friday

is pay day, every week

Runs from Friday to Thursday are paid by bank transfer the next Friday. Every run is listed.

Paradise Sea Express · For captains & boat owners · 4

This is the slide that matters most. 75% of the fare, 100% of tips, paid weekly. Discounts from cruise-ship promo codes come out of Paradise’s share, not the captain’s: your 75% is worked out on the full fare. VAT is charged to the passenger on top of the fare, so it doesn’t come out of your share either.
