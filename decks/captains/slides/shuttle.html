Published one-way fare $10 per person before VAT, boat seating 12, 2 crossings an hour. Your share, before tips. Not a guarantee · 8

The simplest work there is: short crossings, back and forth, on ship days. Each full crossing is $90 to the captain. Both Paradise Island stops (Margaritaville and Carnival) are pick-up points too, so the return leg carries paying passengers. Round-trip tickets are $18 per person ($13.50 to the captain). At 2 crossings an hour, a 6-hour day is 12 crossings: $1,080 with a full boat, $540 half full. A 10-hour day is 20 crossings: up to $1,800. The half-full column is there so nobody feels misled on a slow day.
