Paradise Sea Express · For captains & boat owners · 9

Charters come on top of the fixed-fare runs. Each captain tells us their own hourly rate. When a passenger asks for a boat by the hour, the office books it at that rate, takes payment, then offers it to the captain like any other run. The split is the same: 75% to the captain, Paradise keeps 25%, tips are all the captain’s. Example: at $200 an hour, a 4-hour charter is $800: $600 to the captain.
