Where they’re waiting: when a passenger books with the app open, their phone shares where they’re standing, so the captain knows which end of the dock to pull in at. It’s their position when they booked, not a live feed; the card says if it’s old. Your boat on their map: while a run is on, the app shares the boat’s position, and the passenger’s trip page shows it coming. Captains who prefer can use the captain board in a phone browser instead of installing the app.
