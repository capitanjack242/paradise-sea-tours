    Questions

    Anything else? Ask us in the chat, or call +1 (242) 535-7430.

    How does booking work?

    Pick your pickup, your stop, the day, the time and how many are coming on the booking form. You see the fare straight away. A licensed Bahamian captain nearby takes your run, usually within minutes during operating hours, and you get a private link to your trip.

    When do I pay?

    By card, on our payment provider's secure page, once your captain is confirmed and never before. Fares are per person; 10% Bahamas VAT is added at checkout.

    What is your cancellation policy?

    Free within 2 minutes of booking, or any time before the captain is on the way. A $5 fee once the captain is heading to the pickup dock. No-shows are charged the full fare after a 10-minute wait. No charge if it's on us. Refunds go back to your card within 10 business days.

    What hours do the boats run?

    7am to 10pm, every day. Private charters by appointment.

    Will I be back in time for my ship?

    Tell us your ship and its all-aboard time in the notes when you book, and choose a return time that leaves room to spare. We plan your run around it.

    Can we drink on the boat?

    Yes. Bring your own drinks, and your own cooler if you like; mention coolers in the notes when you book.

    Can I bring my dog?

    Pets are at the captain's discretion. Mention your pet in the notes when you book so the captain knows before confirming.

    What if the weather is bad?

    The captain makes the final call on sea conditions on the day. If we cancel for weather or sea conditions, there's no charge.

    How many people can come?

    Up to 12 on the booking form. For bigger groups, call or WhatsApp +1 (242) 535-7430.

    How much is a private charter?

    Charters are priced for your day: tell us the day, the hours and your group size and we send a fixed price before you pay anything. 2-hour minimum, captain included.
