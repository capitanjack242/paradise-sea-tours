How a run works

Four steps from booking to pay day

1

They book

A passenger picks the trip online or in our app and sees the fare up front.

2

We ask you

Between 7am and 10pm, the run lands on your phone with an alert: who, where, when, how many.

3

You say yes

Tap yes or no within 5 minutes. Nothing is promised to the passenger until a boat agrees.

4

Run it, get paid

Pick them up, take them out, mark it done. Paid that Friday week.

The deal

Simple terms, in writing, on your phone

75%

of every fare is yours

Paradise keeps 25% for marketing, card payments and the office. Your fuel and running costs come out of your 75%.

100%

of every tip is yours

No commission and no VAT taken from tips, ever. Passengers can tip in the app after the ride.

Friday

is pay day, every week

Runs from Friday to Thursday are paid by bank transfer the next Friday. Every run is listed.

Questions captains ask

Straight answers

Who sets the price?

The published fare list. No haggling, for you or the passenger.

Does a promo code cut my pay?

No. Paradise pays every discount. Your share is on the full fare.

What if I can’t take a run?

Say no, or let it lapse after 5 minutes. It goes to another boat.

Can I query my pay?

Yes. You and the office see the same list of runs behind every Friday payment.

Do I pay to join?

No. Joining is free. Paradise only earns when you run a trip.

Can I keep my own customers?

Yes. Switch our app off and run your own passengers as you always have.
