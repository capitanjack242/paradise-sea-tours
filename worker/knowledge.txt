PARADISE SEA EXPRESS — WHAT THE ASSISTANT KNOWS
(Everything the "Ask us" chat on paradiseseaexpress.com may say about the business. The live
fare table is added below this at the time of each question, from the price list. Keep this

WHO WE ARE
- Paradise Sea Express runs on-demand boat rides around Nassau, The Bahamas: fixed per-person
  fares from the Cruise Port (and between stops) to Paradise Island, the beaches and the cays,
  plus private charters by the hour and fishing tours.
- Based at Prince George Wharf, Nassau Harbour (the Nassau Cruise Port).
- Every boat is run by a licensed Bahamian captain.
- Boats run 7am to 10pm, every day. Private charters by appointment.
- Contact: phone or WhatsApp +1 (242) 535-7430, email hello@paradiseseaexpress.com.

HOW BOOKING WORKS
1. Pick your stop: choose where you're being picked up, where you're going, the day, the time
   (Nassau time) and how many are coming, on the booking form on the website (/#book). The
   fare shows straight away.
2. We confirm your captain: a licensed captain nearby takes your run, usually within minutes
   during operating hours. You get a private link to your trip.
3. Pay and ride: pay by card once your captain is confirmed (never before), then meet your
   captain at the dock.
- Trip types: one way, round trip (we come back for you at the time you choose), or a private
  charter.
- Up to 12 passengers per booking on the form; for bigger groups, call or message us.
- No account is needed. Your trip page is reached by its private link: don't share it.
- Notes on the form are for coolers, bags, special stops, the name of your cruise ship, and the
  like.
- Sharing your location when you book (in the app) is optional and only helps the captain find
  you at the dock.
- The Paradise Sea Express app is coming soon; live GPS tracking of your boat comes with it.

FARES AND PAYMENT
- Fares are fixed and per person, shown before you book: no haggling. Prices are in US dollars
  (the Bahamian dollar is the same value).
- 10% Bahamas VAT is added at checkout, on top of the fares listed.
- Round trip fares are per person for both ways.
- Payment is by card, on our payment provider's secure page (Fygaro), once the captain is
  confirmed. We never ask for card details in chat or by phone.
- The fare table below is the price list. Quote fares only from it. If a route isn't in it, say
  the fare is quoted when you book, or call us.

STOPS
Pick-up and drop-off: Nassau Cruise Port; Paradise Island – Margaritaville; Paradise Island –
Carnival; Señor Frog's; Green Parrot; Poop Deck; Fort Montagu; Junkanoo Beach; Arawak Cay / Fish
Fry; Goodman's Bay; Breezes; Baha Mar.
Drop-off only (book a round trip to be collected again): Pearl Island; Floating Bar; Blue
Lagoon; Pigs Beach (Athol Island); Rose Island – Goodies.
Somewhere else: choose "Other" on the form and say where in the notes, or call us.

About the stops:
- Paradise Island (Margaritaville or Carnival): straight across the harbour from the Cruise
  Port, no bridge traffic.
- Pigs Beach (Athol Island): meet the famous swimming pigs, then back to town on your schedule.
- Pearl Island: a private-island beach day just off Nassau, north of Athol Island, with white
  sand and clear water.
- Blue Lagoon (Salt Cay): calm lagoon beaches and dolphin encounters, a short run across the
  water.
- Floating Bar: The Hideaway Floating Bar & Grill, just off Athol Island.
- Rose Island – Goodies: Goodies Beach Bar at the west end of Rose Island; quiet beaches and
  snorkelling away from the crowds.
- West along the shore from the port: Junkanoo Beach, Arawak Cay / Fish Fry, Goodman's Bay,
  Breezes and Baha Mar (Cable Beach). East: Green Parrot, Poop Deck, Fort Montagu.
- Each stop has its own page: /boat-to/<stop>/ (see LINKS).

PRIVATE CHARTERS (by the hour)
- Book the whole boat: your route, your schedule. 2-hour minimum, captain included.
- 6-guest boat (center console, up to 6 guests): harbour and island hops, snorkel stops.
- 8-guest cruiser (seating and a cooler, up to 8 guests): beach days and cays, sunset cruises.
  Best for groups.
- Luxury charter (premium, up to 12 guests): events and celebrations.
- Charter prices are quoted for you: tell us the day, the hours and your group size (on the
  booking form, choose Charter, or call) and we send a fixed price before you pay anything.

FISHING TOURS
- Reef fishing: bottom fishing on the reefs for snapper, grouper and more; great for families
  and first-timers. Half day.
- Deep-sea / offshore: troll the deep blue for mahi, tuna, wahoo and marlin. Half day.
- Sunset and flats: evening reef trips and bonefishing on the flats.
- Rods, bait and licences included; bring your own cooler. Full-day trips available;
  catch-and-cook arranged on request. Prices are quoted for you.

CAPTAINS
- Own a boat in Nassau? Captains can join the Paradise Sea Express network: a steady stream of
  paying passengers and a dispatch system, while staying independent and keeping the majority of
  every fare. Email hello@paradiseseaexpress.com (see /#captains).

DRINKS ON BOARD
- Yes, you can drink on the boat.
